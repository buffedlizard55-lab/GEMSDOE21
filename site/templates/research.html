05 / LEAKAGE & PROMOTION RULES

The protocol is not rewritten after a loss

  1. Global 8-connected known raster components; withhold an entire component if any part intersects a fold. Train outside the geographic quadrant and a 15px Euclidean buffer around the quadrant and every withheld component.
  2. 31 label-free control channels, plus 3 registered co-orientation features. No full-catalogue distances, external fault-line maps, trained context fields, thermal orphan flags or positive template values.
  3. Same 30k P / 100k U samples in paired fits. nnPU prior .03, L2 .001, arcsinh/standardized linear+squared features, max 150 optimizer iterations. U includes positive pixels and is never assigned confirmed-negative targets.
  4. Fixed Hessian across-strike thinning and 2.5% budget on physically supported cells. Dense/sparse evaluation uses the same predictions; sparse retains 20% of whole component IDs as reference and neutralizes the rest pixel-exactly.
  5. Promotion requires +.003 dense gain, positive sparse gain, ≥3/4 dense wins, recovery guard, corrected p<.05, convergence and a reproducible clean current-best comparator. H21-1 fails; H19-4 refit is missing.
  6. No live leaderboard variants, tuning, slot spending or a pretrained historical map masquerading as OOF.

Raster connectivity is a segment proxy, not original geological IDs. Hidden expert label selection is undisclosed. SCAR/prior assumptions, adjacent-block dependence, CPU capacity and mirror provenance limit interpretation.

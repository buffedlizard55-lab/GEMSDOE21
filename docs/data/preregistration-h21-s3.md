## Sources read before registration; what they actually support

1. **USGS OFR 76-260**, Griscom & Conradi (1976), preliminary interpretation, gravity section and magnetic profiles section: https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1976/0260/report.pdf . Magnetic rough-to-smooth changes were interpreted as sudden bedrock-depth changes; the authors explicitly mention magnetic boulders and man-made anomalies. Small positive gravity highs were *probably* due to alluvial cementation. This preliminary case study motivates H21-9/10, not a validated modern transform or forecast. No old study coordinates will be used as labels or prediction priors.
2. **USGS Grauch & Hudson (2007)**: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/guides-understanding-aeromagnetic-expression-faults-sedimentary-basins-lessons-learned . Juxtaposed strata, thickness/depth/susceptibility and topography govern magnetic expression; nonfault ambiguity remains.

## H21-9 fixed prototype contract (synthetic-only this session)

This is a deterministic physical transform, not a fitted model or an outcome. Do not compute field maps and inspect them as a covert validation.

* On float numeric magnetic values, invalid/sentinel cells remain missing. No labels, fault coordinates, catalogue-derived predictor, heat point distance, pretrained full-catalogue field, unit ID or template positive values.
* Normalized Gaussian smoothing (truncate 4) at sigma 1 and 3 pixels, require at least .99 valid support, mode constant (NO reflected evidence beyond a survey boundary). High-pass h = smooth_1 - smooth_3.
* RMS texture = sqrt(normalized 5×5 local mean(h²)). Constant fields and linear ramps have zero interior high-pass/texture; offset/sign reversal leave strength unchanged. Positive-amplitude and neighbour-support guards.
* Gradient normal of smoothed RMS texture (sigma 1); do not choose a direction from zero amplitude. Sample texture at offsets ±2 and ±5 pixels along this normal. Signed contrast = (right-left)/(right+left) when sum>1e-12. Strength uses absolute contrast, min near/far magnitude and same sign; uniform roughness is not a side break.
* Sample near contrast at ±5 pixels along strike using the centre normal. Require same signed transition on both flanks; strength is centre magnitude × minimum flank agreement × log1p(mean side texture). Output one strength plus signed contrast and a separate actual support mask; unsupported output is zero, not a confident negative. No score-dependent reweighting, scale sweep or data-dependent boundary threshold.
* Synthetic invariants and invalid-input/boundary tests must pass before field use. Synthetic success is NOT support for the geological hypothesis or DTI forecast. This prototype has no evaluator/export-to-submission path.
